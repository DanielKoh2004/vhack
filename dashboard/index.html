    Privacy & Compliance Center

    PDPA / GDPR aligned • Data protection for the unbanked

    🔐

    PII Hashing

    All personally identifiable information (sender/recipient IDs) is hashed with SHA-256 + salt before it reaches the ML models. The model never sees raw identities.

    hashlib.sha256(value + salt).hexdigest()[:16]
    📊

    Differential Privacy

    Aggregate dashboard exports use Laplace noise (ε=5.0) to prevent individual transaction inference from summary statistics.

    noisy_score = score + Laplace(0, 1/ε)
    🚫

    Zero Raw PII in Models

    Identity columns (name_sender, name_recipient, transaction_id) are dropped during preprocessing — the model is trained and infers without any identifying data.

    df.drop(['name_sender', 'name_recipient'], axis=1)
    📜

    PDPA & GDPR Ready

    One-way hashing supports right-to-erasure. No reversible encryption means no risk of PII reconstruction even if models are compromised.

    SHA-256 is irreversible → compliant by design
    🌏

    SDG 8.10 — Financial Inclusion for All

    Our privacy-first design ensures unbanked and underserved populations in ASEAN can adopt digital wallets without fear of data exploitation. By cryptographically protecting identities before they touch any AI system, we lower the trust barrier that prevents financial inclusion. The combination of explainable decisions (SHAP), low false-positive rates (0%), and sub-100ms latency means legitimate users are never wrongfully blocked — a critical requirement for populations who depend on every transaction.
